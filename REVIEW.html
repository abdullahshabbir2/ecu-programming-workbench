Implementation architecture

The client and virtual ECU exchange actual simulated CAN frames. Service responses are reconstructed by the transport, rather than passed directly between the client and ECU.

Executed programming trace

Derived from the recorded virtual-bench run. The pause near the beginning is the bounded retry after a deliberately dropped transfer acknowledgement. Timing is simulated, not a hardware throughput measurement.

Engineering decisions

DecisionImplemented behavior
Do not damage the selected bankWrite and verify an inactive bank before committing an active pointer.
Treat retries by operationRetry one exact transfer block; never blindly repeat erase or reset.
Keep diagnostics observableRead DTCs before and after programming and require equality in the nominal update.
Reject invalid conditionsVoltage, engine-running, sequence, size, integrity and version gates.
Bound waitingTransport deadlines, session expiry, pending-response limit and overall request deadline.
